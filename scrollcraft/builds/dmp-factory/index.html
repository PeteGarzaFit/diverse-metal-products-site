Then a press gives it a third dimension.

Progressive, deep-drawn and fine-blanking stamping convert the flat cut blank into a dimensional part, stainless, brass, copper, aluminum, steel or nickel, using DMP's dies or a customer's own tooling after it clears our 14-point inspection.

  • Progressive — one strip, multiple stations, piercing, forming, extruding and drawing in a single pass through the press.
  • Deep drawn — a shape whose depth exceeds half its diameter, drawn from flat sheet in one die.
  • Fine blanking — a clean, square-edged cut face that needs no secondary finishing.
  • Value added — painting, grinding & finishing, wet sanding, plating and polishing, added only where a job needs it.
