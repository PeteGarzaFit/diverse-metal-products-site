TruLaser 1030 · fiber laser cutting

Scroll, and the laser cuts it.

One automated pass, one-off prototype or full production run, same machine, same tolerance every time.

Then a press gives it a third dimension.

Progressive, deep-drawn and fine-blanking stamping convert the flat cut blank into a dimensional part, stainless, brass, copper, aluminum, steel or nickel, using DMP's dies or a customer's own tooling after it clears our 14-point inspection.

  • Progressive — one strip, multiple stations, piercing, forming, extruding and drawing in a single pass through the press.
  • Deep drawn — a shape whose depth exceeds half its diameter, drawn from flat sheet in one die.
  • Fine blanking — a clean, square-edged cut face that needs no secondary finishing.
  • Value added — painting, grinding & finishing, wet sanding, plating and polishing, added only where a job needs it.

Questions

Before you send a print

What file formats do you accept?

.dxf, .dwg, .iges and .pdf. Customers are also welcome to load their CAD files directly into our system, which cuts programming time and keeps the part honest to the print.

What materials can you work with?

Stainless steel, carbon steel, aluminum, copper, brass, nickel and plastic sheet, cut, stamped and finished in-house.

Is there a minimum order size?

We run small runs and large, from a single prototype to full production. Tell us what you need when you request a quote.

Do you handle finishing too?

Yes: painting, grinding & finishing, wet sanding, plating and polishing, added only where a job needs it.

Can you work to defense or DFARS specifications?

Yes. We build parts such as oxygen scrubbers and burners to DFARS and MTR specification today.

How do I send a print for a quote?

Tap Request a quote. It opens an email with a short checklist: attach your print, fill in the blanks and send. We review the print and get back to you with a quote.
